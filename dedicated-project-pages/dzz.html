Overview

Dropzone Z is an endless, round-based action game built in Unity for Android. Each round you fight through waves of enemies to reach delivery zones while dodging meteors that mark their impact point before landing. Between rounds you pick one of three upgrade cards, and the difficulty ramps up the longer you last. I designed and built everything: gameplay, procedural level generation, UI, save system and progression.

Highlights

  • Procedurally generated city levels with roads, grass tiles and delivery spots
  • Endless mode with a round-by-round difficulty curve that unlocks enemies and meteors as you progress
  • Meteor system with warning markers, fair-dodge checks and tiered spawn patterns
  • Roguelike upgrade cards between rounds: damage, speed, extra hearts, double coins and more
  • Adaptive "flow" system that nudges difficulty based on how well the player is doing
  • Left and right handed support, with customizable control position, size and opacity
  • Retention features: daily streak, daily quests, loot boxes and a weapon unlock goal bar
