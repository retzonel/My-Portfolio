Privacy Policy

How our games handle your data, ads and consent choices.

Last updated: October 30, 2026

1. Who we are

This Privacy Policy applies to the mobile games and apps published by Retzonel (rtzn.l) ("we", "us", "our"), including DropZone Z (together, the "Apps"). If you have any questions, contact us at nwochagodswill@gmail.com.

2. Information we collect

We do not require an account and we do not directly ask you for your name, email address, phone number or location.

Game data stored on your device

Your progress (such as coins, diamonds, unlocks, upgrades, settings, statistics and high scores) is saved locally on your device. We do not receive this data on our servers.

Data collected automatically by advertising partners

The Apps show ads through Yodo1 MAS, a mediation platform, which works with third-party ad networks. When ads are loaded or shown, these partners may collect and process:

  • Your device's advertising identifier (Google Advertising ID on Android, IDFA on iOS)
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it
  • Device and software information (model, operating system, language, screen size, network type)
  • App usage and ad interaction data (ads shown, clicked, watched)
  • Crash and performance diagnostics

This data is collected by those partners under their own privacy policies. We do not control it.

In-app purchases

If the App offers in-app purchases, they are processed by Google Play or the Apple App Store. We never receive or store your payment card details.

3. How the information is used

  • To show ads, including personalised ads where you have agreed to them, and to keep the Apps free
  • To measure ad performance and prevent fraud
  • To keep the Apps stable and fix problems
  • To comply with legal obligations

We do not sell your personal information to anyone ourselves.

4. Consent and your choices

  • Consent dialog: On first launch the App may show a privacy and consent dialog. In the EEA, UK and similar regions it uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) so you can accept or decline personalised ads. You can change your choice later from the privacy settings option in the App's settings, where available.
  • iOS tracking: On iOS you can allow or deny tracking in the system prompt, and change it any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Android: You can reset or delete your advertising ID in Settings → Privacy → Ads, and opt out of ads personalisation there.
  • Delete local data: Uninstalling the App removes the game data stored on your device.

5. Sharing with third parties

Information is shared with our advertising partners as described above (Yodo1 and the ad networks it works with, which may include Google AdMob, AppLovin, Unity Ads and others). These partners may be located outside your country. Each acts under its own privacy policy. Details of the networks used are available in Yodo1's documentation.

6. Children

The Apps are not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information through our Apps, contact us and we will help remove it.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in the EEA/UK or the CCPA/CPRA in California), you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal data held about you, to object to or restrict processing, to withdraw consent, and to opt out of the "sale" or "sharing" of personal information for advertising. Because most data is held by our advertising partners, you can exercise these rights through the consent controls described above, with the partners directly, or by contacting us and we will help where we can.

8. Data retention and security

Game data stays on your device until you delete it or uninstall the App. Data held by advertising partners is kept according to their own retention policies. We take reasonable measures to protect information, but no method of storage or transmission is completely secure.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top shows the latest version. Continued use of the Apps after a change means you accept the updated policy.
